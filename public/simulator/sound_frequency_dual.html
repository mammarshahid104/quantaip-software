⚠ Human hearing range is roughly 20 Hz – 20,000 Hz (varies by age). Start with headphones/speakers at low volume, especially above 10 kHz, and when playing two tones together.
When two tones play together, you hear a single pulsing loudness at the beat frequency = |fA − fB|. Try Tone A at 440 Hz and Tone B at 444 Hz — you'll hear a clear "wah-wah-wah" 4 times a second. Set both to the exact same frequency and the beating disappears (0 Hz). This is how musicians tune instruments by ear.
